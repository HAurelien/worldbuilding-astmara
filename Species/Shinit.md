## Basic Information

### Anatomy

They are about 1m tall, and have a disproportionately small chest. They also have four members and uses them similarly as the [[Human]] race, however they are as long as [[Human]] one, which makes them more or less looks like arachnids. However, as their species ended very evolved, there are not many representative of their race use their biological body, most of them got technologically enhanced.

### Genetics and Reproduction

They are hermaphrodite but their reproduction system is similar to the [[Human]] one.

### Growth Rate & Stages

Their body is fully grown after about 3 years, however, their brain takes about 5 to be at it's optimal stage. They remain very healthy until around 15, afterward, at some point, their body get overrun by the plant they use to produce photosynthesis, and so they actually die from the plants replacing so much of their blood that they do not feed their muscles and brains quickly enough. Which usually ends up in a heart attack.
However, if they do not share their body with plants, they can live up to 40 years for the oldest.

### Dietary Needs and Habits

They need to eat more or less constantly, as their stomach is never really stopping from digesting. However, they can only eat small portions at a time, as their stomach is pretty tiny.

## Additional Information

### Social Structure

They have been through a lot of different social structure during their history, but it has mostly been some kind of pyramidal scheme with elections.

### Facial characteristics

Their face was reduced to the minimum : a pair of eyes, a little hole for their nose, a pair of ears and a rather under proportionate mouth.

### Average Intelligence

Their average intelligence is about twice the one of the [[Human]]. And it is actually not fluctuating much depending on the individual, as the race has been very sharpened by it's history.

### Perception and Sensory Capabilities

Similar to human's. However, they see much more green than red as [[Human]] do.

### Symbiotic and Parasitic organisms

They consumes a rather abondant plant on their planet whenever one of them is pregnant, which is not destroyed by their digestive system, and allow their future child to later on produce photosynthesis.

## Civilization and Culture

### Gender Ideals

There is no gender in their society, as they are hermaphrodites.

### Average Technological Level

They reached a technology level rather insane, given the amount of time they had before their unstable sun burns their whole star system.
Their magical knowledge allowed them to create technologies hybrid between magical and electric, allowing them to create automatized robots feeding from the magic in the air.
They developped a system allowing them to connect themselves to a satellite-based Internet in order to share informations from mind to mind all around the world with a magical device scanning their brain waves and generating illusions to visualise, hear and feel things.

### Major Language Groups and Dialects

They developped multiple dialects through the age, however they stopped using them around a hundred years before the end of their civilisation, communicating through devices able to read their brain waves, allowing a much more efficient communication. However, writing were still produced, especially in order to communicate to other civilisations they might cross through the Universe.

### Common Etiquette Rules

The current etiquette does not matter much, as only a few groups of tenth of survivors managed to survive the explosion of their star. So it will depends on which group we speak about, but as a very curious and uninterested species, they do not matter much about it.

### Common Dress Code

Most of them replaced more or less entirely their body with mechanical parts, so there is no such things as "dress code".

### Culture and Cultural Heritage

Most of it was lost after their civilisation got almost extinct, and the few survivors floating in space or who crashed rather safely on a planet value it a lot. Most of them swore to keep on track and save their civilisation, however, as all mecanical entities, they cannot reproduce anymore, so it has been rather unsuccessfull for most of them.

### Common Taboos

Giving up, suicide. Those who decided to go for the stars, even if this would mean waiting for hundred of years in the nothingness, hoping they will survive the blast, swore that as long as they would live, they would use this chance to save their people.

### History

Their species have been appearing and disappearing a few time before they finally reached a technological level high enough to survive their own magic production. No real huge ever surges through their history, as the technology has been rather badly understood for a long time, and as their physical abilities were really limited. So they have always been very kind and helpful to each others in order to survive.
Violence appeared once the technology has been mastered and the AMP project finally installed the dimentional cathalyser. However, they quicly developped a real fear from their star, as it has been determined it was close from implosing to a supernova. The afterward period has been rather troubled, until a great mathematician defined a plan to save their civilisation. Their whole world was then turned toward science, seeing there a chance to survive if they all forcused their efforts toward it.
Since, their civilisation has been in peace. Stricts rulers were elected, and science finished by covering almost all of their needs, so the inner tensions were strongly reduced.
In the end, a fleet of ten ships left their planet, with as much people and knowlege from their civilisation as possible. All hoping to survive.
However, as planned, they were a few thousand years short, and when their shields eventually collapsed, most of the fleet was lost. Only a few experimental cabins survived, using an utterly complicated magical system to blink out from our plane. After a few days of intensive work, and a lot of sacrifices, some of them managed to reactivate their life support system. Doomed to wait for a planet to attract them in it's gravitational field, they all entered in cryogenic capsules, while the fully robotics one waited to prepare for the crash.
From the billions they were, around a thousand were equipped with the plane blinking system, and from this thousand, around a hundred survived up to this point. The others got malfunctions, never blinked, or never managed to reactivate their life support in time.

### Historical Figures

Two major historical figures were really distinguished from their civilisation. We'll call them Mark Patterson and Henry Rutherford.
Mark Patterson was the person in charge of the A.M.P., the anti-magic project. It was created in order to destroy magic for their people to be able not to die from the magic overflow on their planet.
Henry Rutherford was a very famous mathematician, who designed the U.T.S., the Universe Terraforming System, which allowed them to prepare very early the universe for the day they would finally have the technology to leave their planet from the nearly-dying star.

### Interspecies Relations and Assumptions

Actually, they were the only intelligent species of they home world. And for now, it hasn't been decided that they encountered any other intelligent species since.

## Average Physique

They are rather weak however. It appears that an important digestive system is very dangerous whenever magic is overflowing. However, they actually are enduring, able to walk for a long time without stopping.

## Body Tint, Colouring and Marking

They are mostly green, as they seems to be able to partially generate ATP from photosynthesis.
