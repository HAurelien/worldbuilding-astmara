Eyes being too big, she decided to face the [[Lower gods|lower gods]] and ended up locked up in an amulet within the hands of [[Angres Rimas]].

In the [[Ether]], she begun absorbing people's magic, more refined and contained than the one running free in the [[Ether]]. At some point, she attacked some lower gods, protected by the [[God of Eldritch creatures]] and the [[God of Progress]] she had a pact with. However, once everyone understood what she was doing, they all teamed up to stop her. Or at least, almost all, as the [[God of Eldritch creatures]] didn't cared about it.
After a lot of sacrifices, they managed to exploit a recent experiment from a few [[Human]] researchers, [[Angres Ivan]] and [[Angres Adelaïde|Angres Adélaïde]], and threw her away through the [[Pillar]] within the [[Human]] realm, in [[Aethernam]]. However, she apparently kept her magical abilities even within the human realm, and because of that she threatened to destroy the wold if they wouldn't let her absorb their power. And they declined. So, she destroyed it, as a display of her ability. She also used cursed the wold in diffrent way doing this. However, she didn't knew the [[Pillar]] she used to transfert the magic she had shared a shard of it's essence within a young boy, [[Angres Rimas]], whose [[Ether mirror]] got quickly located, and used to break through [[Arrack]]'s defenses unnoticed as a lot of magic was flowing through him. Using this, they managed to seal her in an amulet made from half of the essence of [[Angres Rimas]], allowing it to have the magical capacities to seal her away.
Afterward, [[Angres Rimas]] got in charge of taking care of [[Arrack]].
