The day the Great King Absalom got crowned. It was used as the year 0 since then in honor to him, and to show the ceisure of the World before and after his reign, and his ascention.

On the day of the 12/04/0, the Great King Absalom the first, king of the last country of Heliosia, got crowned, after more than three hundred years of an unceasing war. Since the early years of the ruling of Bellator the fourth, almost all the world had been conquered. People living in fear of being requisitioned to a new war against a still unknown opponent, living in poor condition as all the country's resources were deployed to shed more blood.
But during this early day of spring, a new king got crowned. A new ruler everyone shall remember. He was so powerful, and yet so kind, that he managed to, in only 40 years, bring the world to peace and abundance. But he had so much to do, that he neglected both his wife and his two children. Or, at least, one of them. He raised his first child to be a ruler, like him, cultivating against his own will the hatred of his second child, which were sent to see the world and take care of secondhand problems between different culture. The day the Great King Absalom died, was a tragedy for the whole world, and there were heavy suspicions that he passed away from poison. His two sons argued afterward, once saying that the world should stay united under the same banner, the first child, and the other that they should restore the world as it was before the war. And it took not long for the world to fall apart again. The both sons rallying partisans, they ended up splitting the world in two. And this argument quickly escalated to a new war. And the day the two armies, the army of Absalom the Second and the one Samar the fifth were about to clash, facing each other in a field with thousands of person in each camp, a lone voice echoes through the field." I, Absalom the First, prohibit you to face each other on the field of war. I, Absalom the First, prohibit you to shred blood on the land of our country. I, Absalom the First, shall ensure the well-being of what was build by hatred and death, and was preserved through love and forgiveness. I understand the reasons you are about to fight for, my sons, but I shall inquire you to cease this foolishness at once. Heliosia will survive this crisis, and keep all the people united. And to those who do not want to follow my guidance, you should fear my wrath. Now, I, Absalom the First, have spoken."
