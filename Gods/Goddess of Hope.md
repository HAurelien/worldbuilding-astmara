The "Goddess of Hope", who actually changed her name from "Goddess of Illusion", is prayed by most of the thieves and scammers. She also have a few fanatics, which are mostly chased for their evil deeds. She is actually rather found of [[Arrack]] for her rather.. cunning mind. She is the one who helped her breaking free from the [[Lone Protectors]] when they binded her to the Amulet.
 
First a great, powerful sorceress, praised by her peers. However, after she ascended as a God, she slowly started to discover how incapable humans were. How weak-minded they were, how evil they were to those lower than them (feminism in middle-age but with a way to change things), something she never witnessed because of how skilled she was as a human. Slowly but steadily, she convinced herself that humans were to disappear, and the sooner the better. Hence her unofficial alliance with [[Arrack]] against the [[Lone Protectors]], even if she does it mostly from the shadows. Among the [[Lower gods]], despite not being the most powerful, she is one of the most feared alongside with the [[God of Progress]] for her abilities, wit, and determination.
