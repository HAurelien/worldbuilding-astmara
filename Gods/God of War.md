This God ascended after the Cataclysm, in 2954. Dying during a battle where his group was at 1 against 10, he turned to a God after having been accounted for the real bloodshed of the battle was in the creature side. Local legend says that the nearby area was imbued with magic for a whole year after the enemies were crushed.
He very little involve with the [[Lone Protectors]], taking [[Angres Rimas]] accountable for the state of [[Aethernam]] still being so bad after the Great Cataclysm despite the fact he believes [[Angres Rimas]] could easily fend off the creatures and free the world.
